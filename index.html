Welcome to My Marketing Portfolio

My name is Maggie Johnston, and I am a marketing student passionate about digital marketing, social media, content creation, branding, and communication. Through my academic experience and professional opportunities, I have developed a strong interest in creating marketing strategies that connect organizations with their audiences.

I have developed experience in the digital marketing field, working with clients across diverse industries including sports, insurance, nonprofit, agriculture, tourism, and community organizations. These experiences have helped me understand how marketing strategies can be adapted to different audiences, industries, and goals.

My experience has also allowed me to work with social media, content creation, event promotion, community engagement, branding, and digital communication. I enjoy combining creativity with marketing strategy to develop content that is both visually engaging and meaningful to an audience.

My Marketing Experience

One of the most valuable parts of my experience has been working with local organizations and businesses. Through these opportunities, I have created social media content, promoted events, developed marketing materials, and helped organizations communicate their stories. These experiences have strengthened my communication skills and given me a better understanding of how marketing can support a community.

My Approach

I believe effective marketing should be creative, strategic, and authentic. Whether I am creating social media content, developing a campaign, promoting an event, or working on a branding project, I focus on understanding the audience and creating a message that encourages people to connect.
