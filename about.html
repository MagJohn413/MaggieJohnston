A bit more about me

I'm a senior at Iowa State University studying Marketing. I started my education at DMACC, where I played volleyball and earned my associate's degree in business, then transferred to Iowa State to finish my bachelor's degree.

I currently work part-time with Visit Boone County, where I focus on marketing, communications, and event promotion — getting hands-on experience with things like bus tours, RAGBRAI, and all things tourism. Outside of that, I've picked up marketing, design, and social media experience across a handful of organizations, from a community center to an agriculture technology company.

I enjoy creating engaging content, connecting with people, and sharing stories in creative ways — whether that's a flyer, a newsletter, a social post, or a short video.
