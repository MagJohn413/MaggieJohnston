About Me

Welcome to my website! My name is Maggie Johnston, and I am a senior studying Marketing at Iowa State University. I currently work as a Marketing Coordinator with Visit Boone County, where I support tourism initiatives, community engagement, and large‑scale event marketing. This website highlights my work, my experience, and the projects I’ve contributed to throughout my time in marketing and tourism. My goal is to create meaningful connections, promote local experiences, and help communities grow through strategic marketing, storytelling, and thoughtful design.

During my time with Visit Boone County, I’ve had the opportunity to work on major tourism events such as RAGBRAI, the Farm Progress Show, and multiple bus tours that bring visitors from across the country. These events have strengthened my understanding of tourism marketing, event coordination, and the importance of creating memorable experiences for both residents and visitors. Whether I’m developing social media content, designing promotional materials, or helping coordinate event logistics, I enjoy being part of the process that brings people together and showcases what makes Boone County special.

Marketing has always been something I’ve been passionate about, especially when it involves community‑focused projects. I love creating lifestyle content, building brand stories, and designing visuals that communicate a clear message. My work often includes photography, video editing, social media strategy, and digital content creation. I believe that strong marketing is rooted in authenticity, clarity, and connection—three values I bring into every project I work on.

This website serves as a portfolio of my work and a place where I can share my experience in tourism marketing, event promotion, and content creation. You’ll find examples of projects I’ve contributed to, services I offer, and ways to connect with me for future opportunities. My goal is to continue growing as a marketer, expanding my skills, and supporting organizations that value creativity, community, and meaningful engagement.

Thank you for visiting my site and taking the time to learn more about my work. Whether you’re here to explore my portfolio, connect about a project, or simply learn more about Boone County tourism, I’m glad you’re here. Feel free to reach out through my contact page or submit the form provided. I look forward to connecting with you and continuing to build a career centered around marketing, tourism, and community impact.
